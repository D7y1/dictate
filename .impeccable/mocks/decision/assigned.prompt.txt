Visual world: edit-bay hardware. Warm neutral-gray machine panel (#2e2e2c and #1f1f1e), ivory key legends (#e9e6de), amber (#e8a23a) used only for marks and timecode, red (#d8382f) used only for the REC tally and the cursor. Matte, flat, precise hardware detailing: keycap edges, hairline panel seams. Typography: a crisp grotesk in small caps for labels, monospace for timecode and event numbers. A neutral gray surround so nothing tints the footage. No glow, no neon, no gradients, no glassmorphism. It must read instantly as a professional tool for reviewing a video by pointing and talking.
