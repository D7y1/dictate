# Code of Conduct

Dictate follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, and keep criticism about the work, not the person. Harassment, insults, discrimination, and publishing someone's private information are not welcome in issues, pull requests, discussions, or anywhere else this project lives.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer, [@D7y1](https://github.com/D7y1), privately through the contact details on their GitHub profile, or use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may remove comments, code or issues that break these rules, and may block people who keep breaking them, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
